# Assistant Catalogue, Competitor Rules & Honesty (Miraside)

Read before classifying a lead or picking competitors.

## The assistant catalogue (fixed)

Pick exactly ONE per lead. The outcome line is hard-coded — never reword it.

| key | outcome line | choose when |
|---|---|---|
| `quote_form` | replies to your website quote requests in minutes instead of hours | the business takes quote/contact-form requests — trades, home/field services, agencies, B2B services, dealerships |
| `email_inquiry` | replies to new email enquiries instantly and routes them to the right person | high inbound email volume / a shared inbox (info@, sales@, support@) that needs triage + routing |
| `invoice` | reads invoices from email and sends everything to accounting automatically | heavy supplier/AP invoice volume — distribution, wholesale, construction, manufacturing, multi-location ops |
| `expense` | categorises receipts and expenses automatically for accounting | lots of staff receipts/expenses — field teams, sales-heavy orgs, professional services |
| `cv_candidate` | reviews incoming CVs and highlights the strongest candidates automatically | recruiting/staffing agencies, or any company hiring at volume (lots of inbound CVs) |

**Default:** if the website + LinkedIn don't clearly point to one, choose `quote_form` — almost every services business has a slow quote/contact form.

## ICP reference (for the serve/skip verdict)

**Serve** — services- and operations-heavy businesses, roughly **$2M–$50M revenue, 10+ employees**, where the buyer is the CEO / owner / founder / COO. Broad industries: professional services (law, tax, accounting, audit, wealth/RIA, insurance, mortgage, title/escrow, HR/recruiting, consulting), non-clinical healthcare (multi-location dental, derm, optical, audiology, PT, vet, medical billing), real estate (brokerage & property mgmt, advisory), AEC (architecture, engineering, construction mgmt, GCs, specialty trades), logistics (freight, 3PL, trucking/fleet), distribution/wholesale, retail & multi-store e-comm, hospitality (hotel/restaurant groups, venues, coworking), automotive (dealership groups, powersports, collision, service), education/training, multi-location home/field services (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, pest, cleaning, landscaping, roofing, solar, restoration), marketing agencies.

**Skip** — under $2M / under 10 employees; over ~$50M; pre-PMF startups; distressed/restructuring; lifestyle businesses with no growth intent; pure software/SaaS/AI; top-tier strategy consulting; boutique creative/design studios; pure coaching/concierge; hardware/heavy manufacturing; clinical healthcare, banks, pharma, government, defense, cannabis, adult, gambling, MLM, crypto/Web3; companies with internal AI teams or recently-hired AI leadership.

Decide `icp_verdict` = **serve** (worth contacting) or **skip** (out of ICP) with a one-line reason. When the size is uncertain but the industry clearly fits, lean **serve**.

## Competitor rules

- **Real and direct only.** Same kind of business, same buyer, in the right geography. Grounded in the live search results — never invented or pulled from memory.
- **Geography (set per upload):** US → competitors **based in or strongly serving that state**; Europe → 1–2 **big region-wide** competitors.
- **Exclude:** the prospect itself; directories/aggregators/marketplaces/listicles (Yelp, Clutch, G2, Crunchbase, Wikipedia, "best/top N" pages); generic platforms.
- **Two distinct companies**, named cleanly as a customer would say them.
- If the results are weak, still return the two best-supported direct competitors rather than nothing.

## Honesty

1. Are both competitors **real, direct, and in the right geography** — evidenced by the search results (not guessed)?
2. Is the **assistant a genuine fit** for what they do?
3. Outcome lines and follow-ups are **fixed** — don't embellish or invent results.
4. No **Fundraisr** exposure — never cite Avalanche/Fundraisr clients as Miraside's.
5. The deliverable is built from real evidence — no fabricated client numbers.

## What Miraside is (context for the deliverable)

Miraside builds done-for-you AI assistants/agents integrated into a business's existing tools, operational within ~30 days, with a 30-day money-back guarantee. The cold message offers ONE assistant for free as the entry point; the call (later) is for the full team. Keep the deliverable concrete, finished, and tailored to the prospect.
