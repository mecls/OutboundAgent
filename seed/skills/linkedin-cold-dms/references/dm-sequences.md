## Picking the assistant — quick cues

- "Get a quote" / "request a quote" / contact form on the site, trades/services → `quote_form`.
- Recruiting/staffing, or a careers page with many open roles → `cv_candidate`.
- Shared inbox (info@/sales@), high inbound email → `email_inquiry`.
- Distribution/construction/manufacturing, lots of suppliers → `invoice`.
- Field teams / sales-heavy with staff receipts → `expense`.

## Picking competitors — what good looks like

- Two companies a customer would actually compare them against, in the right geography.
- Clean brand names, not "Top 10 HVAC companies in Texas".
- If the search surfaces only directories, pick the two named companies that appear most consistently inside them.

## Follow-ups (same offer, twice, then stop)

- **Day 3:** Hi {first}, the AI assistant already works and I'm happy to personalise it for you for free. Want me to send it over?
- **Day 7:** Hi {first}, last nudge on this — it's ready whenever you are. Want me to send it over?

## The deliverable (after a yes)

Build the chosen assistant for them, finished and specific: trigger → the exact action in their voice with a realistic worked example → routing/qualifying → output/next step → how it plugs into their tools. Add a short send-with message + one line on why it works.
